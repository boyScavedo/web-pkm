# PKM Research

## What is a PKM

A Personal Knowledge Management system captures, organizes, and retrieves
information for individual use. Unlike team wikis or enterprise CMS, a PKM
optimizes for one person's thinking patterns, not organizational compliance.

## Core principles

1. **Capture fast, organize later.** Friction-free input beats perfect taxonomy.
2. **Markdown is canonical.** Portable, version-controllable, human-readable.
3. **Links create structure.** Wikilinks and backlinks reveal relationships
   that folders alone cannot.
4. **Progressive summarization.** Notes evolve from raw capture to refined
   knowledge over time.
5. **Search over browse.** At scale, search is faster than navigation.
6. **The PKM is the data layer.** Applications consume it; the PKM doesn't
   depend on any application.

## What we studied

- PARA (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives)
- GTD (Getting Things Done)
- Second Brain / Progressive Summarization
- Obsidian architecture and linking model
- Content creation operating systems
- Life OS / goal tracking systems
- Current Next.js + PostgreSQL PKM implementations

## Key insight

No single methodology covers everything. The system combines:
- **PARA** for information classification (where is this useful?)
- **GTD** for task capture and clarification
- **Linked notes** for knowledge relationships
- **Progressive summarization** for knowledge distillation
- **Project management** for execution tracking
- **Creator workflow** for content production

The coherence comes from a shared data layer, not from forcing one methodology
onto every aspect of life.
