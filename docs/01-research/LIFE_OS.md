# Life OS Research

## What Life OS adds beyond PKM + Projects + Content

- **Goals**: What am I trying to accomplish?
- **Areas**: What responsibilities do I maintain?
- **Routines**: What do I do regularly?
- **Reviews**: What should change?
- **Journal**: What happened?
- **Metrics**: What's measurably improving?

## What we take

- **Goals as first-class entities** — linked to projects and notes
- **Areas** map directly to PARA's "areas" category
- **Weekly review** — from GTD, surfaces in dashboard
- **Daily journal** — lightweight, timestamped notes
- **Selected metrics** — only track what drives decisions

## What we skip

- **Habit tracking with streaks/gamification** — unless it directly serves a goal
- **Mood tracking** — no evidence it helps at this scale
- **Complex routine scheduling** — a simple checklist, not a cron system
- **Vanity dashboards** — every metric must have a purpose

## Implementation timing

Phase 14, only after PKM + Projects + Content are stable.
The Life OS is built on top of the PKM, not alongside it.
